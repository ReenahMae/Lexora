# Lexora

A Roblox-based gamified learning system that improves **Grade 5 basic text structure comprehension**, aligned with the **MATATAG** curriculum.

> Capstone project · Team 2526-sem2-it411-12 · BS Information Technology, College of Computer Studies, Cebu Institute of Technology – University (CITU)

**Play it:** Lexora on Roblox(https://www.roblox.com/share?code=7805475715572a45a81541d487193c13&type=ExperienceDetails&stamp=1792239236214) 
**Access:** Public 
## Overview

Lexora teaches students to recognize basic text structures (description, cause and effect, sequence, and more) through exploration, NPC missions, and environmental challenges instead of quizzes. Learning evidence comes from in-game telemetry, such as wrong picks, trap triggers, and first-try versus replay performance.

## Features

- Four themed worlds, each focused on a text structure pattern
- NPC-driven missions with item collection and environmental interaction
- EXP-based progression: EXP unlocks areas inside a world, and completing a world unlocks the next portal
- Stealth assessment through behavioral telemetry (no quiz-type actions)
- In-game **Menu > Analytics** dashboard showing first try vs now per reading skill, challenges, and time per mission


## How to Play

1. Open the experience link and join.
2. Start in **Central Camp** and walk to a world portal.
3. Talk to NPCs (press the ProximityPrompt key) to accept missions.
4. Explore, collect the correct items, and avoid traps.
5. Earn EXP to unlock new areas, then finish the world to unlock the next portal.
6. Check your progress under **Menu > Analytics**.

## Data and Telemetry

Logged through DataStoreService [and synced to Supabase]:

- Mission acceptance and completion
- Correct and incorrect item picks
- Trap triggers and hazards
- Completion times [currently not tracked]
- First playthrough baseline vs replay sessions

No personal information is collected beyond [Roblox user ID].
